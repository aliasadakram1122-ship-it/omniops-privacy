Privacy Policy for OmniOps

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

OmniOps ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a browser extension designed to assist Amazon UK and eBay UK sellers with order fulfillment workflows, profit calculations, product image downloads, privacy screen blurring, and Google Sheets order synchronization.

1. Information We Collect and Use

  • Authentication & Session Data: When you sign in to access sheet synchronization or dashboard features, your email address, session token, and configured Google Sheet / tab settings are stored in chrome.storage.local and sent to your configured Google Apps Script endpoint to authenticate requests.
  • Order & Shipping Information: When you use order capture or address autofill features on eBay UK and Amazon UK pages, order details (such as customer name, shipping address, order IDs, costs, and earnings) are processed on your device to autofill checkout fields or sync rows to your designated Google Sheet.
  • Extension Preferences & Cache: Feature toggles, column mapping order, voice alert settings, and cached dashboard figures are stored locally in chrome.storage.local or page localStorage.

2. How Data Is Shared and Stored

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties or data brokers.
  • Order and dashboard data synced by you is transmitted directly to the Google Apps Script endpoint and Google Sheet that you configure.
  • Product image downloads requested by you are handled directly through Chrome's download manager.

3. Privacy Blur Mode

The privacy blur feature modifies page styling locally in your browser to visually obscure sensitive account, order, or listing information during screen viewing or sharing. It does not transmit blurred content externally.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Local extension data remains in your browser until you clear extension storage, sign out, or uninstall OmniOps. You can manage or remove data synced to your Google Sheet directly within your Google account.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or OmniOps, please contact us via your support or developer contact channel listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
